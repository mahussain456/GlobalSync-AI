# Clockify — Competitor Profile

**Reviewed:** 15 September 2026
**Depth:** Focused public-page review
**Category:** Freelance pricing; adjacent work/billing workflow
**Website:** https://clockify.me/hourly-rate-calculator

## Positioning and capabilities

The free calculator asks about expenses, desired income, holidays, and time off. Supporting instructions explain assumptions and connect the calculation to time tracking and invoicing.

## Access and pricing

Calculator advertised free; broader platform pricing not audited.

## Implication for GlobalSync AI

Let users edit assumptions instead of presenting a universal salary-equivalent multiplier. GlobalSync should carry a calculated rate into its invoice builder. Full product superiority was not assessed.

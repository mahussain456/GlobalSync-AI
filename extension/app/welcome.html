GlobalSync Time Lens

Times, in your cities.

Time Lens is ready. It stays out of the way until you select a time, then shows it in your four cities, with who's at work.

  1. 1
    Select a time on any pageLike “Thursday 3pm EST” or “15:00 London”. A small clock button appears beside it. Click it for the conversion.
  2. 2
    Or right-click itChoose “Convert with Time Lens”. This also works for times without a zone, which are read as your home city's time.
  3. 3
    Pin it and pick your citiesClick the puzzle icon in your toolbar and pin Time Lens. Open it (or press Alt+Shift+T) to convert typed times, choose your cities, or switch on the floating mini clock.

# Every Time Zone

Research date: 2026-09-16. Scope: focused public product and conversion-flow review.

## Category

Direct: visual timezone comparison.

## Observed product behavior

The public page presents a cross-zone time display with link sharing and iCal controls. [Primary source](https://everytimezone.com/).

## Implication for GlobalSync (inference)

Preserve useful state in shared links and offer a portable calendar file after selecting a time.

## Limits and remaining differences

A draggable visual timeline remains a possible later enhancement, subject to usability testing. Traffic, keyword rankings, customer sentiment and paid-plan prices were not independently measured.

Public-page research is preserved in [the dated source capture](raw/2026-09-16/source-pages.json). No Firecrawl or DataForSEO data was available in this session.

# World Time Buddy

Research date: 2026-09-16. Scope: focused public product and conversion-flow review.

## Category

Direct: cross-zone meeting planning.

## Observed product behavior

The feature page describes selectable time intervals, 12/24-hour display, date navigation, DST warnings, calendar export and shareable event pages. [Primary source](https://www.worldtimebuddy.com/features).

## Implication for GlobalSync (inference)

Reduce the steps from choosing cities to sharing an actual meeting. GlobalSync now supports duration, local working hours, copy, a restorable link and calendar drafts.

## Limits and remaining differences

Its richer timeline and calendar overlay remain useful capabilities GlobalSync does not match. Traffic, keyword rankings, customer sentiment and paid-plan prices were not independently measured.

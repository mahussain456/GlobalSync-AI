# Wise Currency Converter

Research date: 2026-09-16. Scope: focused public product and conversion-flow review.

## Category

Adjacent: currency estimates and transfer decisions.

## Observed product behavior

The converter makes amount and currency direction prominent, labels its rate context, and presents historical views and rate-alert entry points. [Primary source](https://wise.com/gb/currency-converter/).

## Implication for GlobalSync (inference)

Keep inputs and displayed results consistent. Include rate source and date in copied results and distinguish reference estimates from provider transfer quotes.

## Limits and remaining differences

GlobalSync does not transfer funds or provide rate alerts. No pricing or transfer-cost comparison was performed. Traffic, keyword rankings, customer sentiment and paid-plan prices were not independently measured.
